# Research Dossier R1 — Regulations 2026–2028

## 1. Power unit & energy (CONFIRMED unless noted)

- 2026 PU: ~50/50 ICE/electrical split. ICE ≈400 kW; MGU-K up to **350 kW** (3× 2025's 120 kW) [S-12, S-05].
- **MGU-H removed**; fuel **100% advanced sustainable** (drop-in; CCU/waste/biomass) [S-05].
- Fuel energy flow: cap **3000 MJ/h**; below 10,500 rpm curve **EF = 0.27·N(rpm) + 165 MJ/h** [S-04 Art. 5.4.3/5.4.4].
- ES SoC window ≤ **4 MJ**; harvest ≤ **9 MJ/lap** from CU-K HV bus; MGU-K absolute DC cap 350 kW [S-04].
- Mass minimums: ICE ≥130 kg; PU ≥185 kg [S-04].
- Deploy limit is speed-dependent (Article C5.2.7 cap 350 kW absolute): normal curve **min(350, 1800−5·v) below 340 km/h**, then **6900−20·v** for 340≤v<345, **0 ≥345 km/h**; Overtake curve **350 kW flat to 337 km/h**, taper to **0 at 355 km/h** [S-41 REPORTED citing Section C Iss 20; article numbers CONFIRMED].
- **Overtake Mode** (DRS replacement): Detection Line + Activation Line; event-published **Detection Gap** (1.0 s in practice 2026); +0.5 MJ harvest (C5.2.10 iii — REPORTED, article confirmed via S-30); disabled under Safety Car (restored after all cars pass post-SC line) and **Low Grip Conditions**; in lap-time-classified sessions enabled+activated for all [S-02/B2b Art. B7.2; S-30; S-31; S-32 REPORTED].
- **Boost**: manual deployment of stored energy, usable anywhere — the defender's counter-tool [S-33, S-32].
- Per-event FIA deployment documents (published ~4 weeks pre-event) set: Detection Gap, line positions, session energy allocations (qualifying 6.0 MJ; FP/out-laps 8.5 MJ — Austria 2026 example), Power Limited Distance (2,923 m), and scenario caps (250 kW in defined zones) [S-42 REPORTED]. Mid-2026: FIA automated qualifying outlap deployment and allowed teams to choose deploy start independent of throttle [S-43 REPORTED]. **Design consequence**: all of these are profile/event data; the kernel has no 2026 constants.

## 2. Chassis & aero (CONFIRMED)

- Min mass **768 kg** (2025: 798 kg incl. driver) [S-11]. Car width **1900 mm** (from 2000) [S-16].
- **Active aero full-time**: front+rear flaps; Straight Mode (open, low drag) / Corner Mode (closed, downforce), zone-based [S-11, S-33]. (Earlier public naming: X-mode/Z-mode [S-11 family].)
- Tyres: 18-inch rims retained; tread **−25 mm front / −30 mm rear**; diameter **−15 mm front / −10 mm rear**; set-of-four ~1.6 kg lighter [S-15, S-16]. Compounds C1–C5 homologated 2025-12-15; wider compound gaps intended for strategy variety [S-17].
- Supplier: Pirelli sole supplier **through 2028** (one-year option exercised 2026-06-11) [S-10, S-14]. Beyond 2028 open tender → 2029+ unknown.
